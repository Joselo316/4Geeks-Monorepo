# HealthCore Digital — Project Brief

## 📋 Business Description

**HealthCore** is an outpatient healthcare clinic network founded in 2011 in Austin, Texas. It operates a network of **12 clinics** distributed across two countries:

- 🇺🇸 **9 clinics in the USA** (Texas, Florida, and Georgia)
- 🇬🇧 **3 clinics in the United Kingdom** (London and Manchester)

### Services Offered
- Primary care
- Specialist consultations
- Chronic disease management
- Preventive health programs

### Organization Scale
- **Employees:** ~200 (clinical staff, operations, administration, growing technology team)
- **Annual Revenue:** ~$28 million USD
- **Organizational Model:** Outpatient ambulatory care (non-hospital)

### Core Mission
Founded by **Dr. Sandra Okonkwo** (MD, CEO) on a simple principle: *quality, accessible healthcare without long wait times or confusing systems*. HealthCore built its reputation on same-day appointments, extended hours, and bilingual staff (in US markets).

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The **HealthCore Digital** unit was created with these strategic goals:

1. **Modernize Technology Infrastructure**

6. **Enable Sustainable Scaling**
   - Build foundations for future clinic expansion
   - Create data-driven decision-making capabilities for leadership

## ⚠️ Problems Being Solved

### 1. **Fragmented Clinical Systems (USA vs UK)**
**Problem:** Two completely disconnected EHR (Electronic Health Record) systems
- USA clinics use one EHR platform
- UK clinics use a different EHR platform
- **Zero communication between systems**
- Patient records do not follow patients who transfer between regions

**Business Impact:**
- Loss of historical context in patient care
- Duplicate tests and procedures
- Safety risks from incomplete medical history

**Solution Area:** Unified clinical API, master patient index, cross-region record access

---

### 2. **Clinical Staff Administrative Burden**
**Problem:** Clinicians spend 35 minutes/day on manual documentation tasks
- Could be significantly reduced with AI-assisted documentation
- Takes away from actual patient care time

**Business Impact:**
- Reduced clinician productivity
- Staff burnout and turnover risk
- Opportunity cost: lost patient throughput

**Solution Area:** AI-assisted clinical documentation, speech-to-text integration, auto-generated summaries

---

### 3. **Critical No-Show Rate (22%)**
**Problem:**
- Current no-show rate is **22%** across the network
- More than **2x the industry average** (8-10%)
- No proactive patient contact system exists

**Business Impact:**
- **Annual cost: ~$1.8M USD** in lost appointment slots
- Poor patient experience and clinical continuity
- Reduced revenue and clinic capacity utilization

**Solution Area:** Unified online booking platform, predictive no-show model, automated reminders (SMS/email/app), intelligent rescheduling

---

### 4. **High Insurance Claim Rejection Rate (14%)**
**Problem:**
- Claim rejection rate is **14%** — more than **2x the industry standard** (5-8%)
- Manual claim submission with inconsistent coding practices across clinics
- No review before submission to flag high-risk claims

**Business Impact:**
- Significant revenue loss due to denied/delayed reimbursement
- Accounts payable bottleneck
- Administrative overhead managing denials and re-submissions

**Solution Area:** AI-assisted claim review, automated coding suggestions, rejection pattern analysis, automated denial follow-up

---

### 5. **Lack of Executive Visibility**
**Problem:**
- Dr. Okonkwo receives weekly reports from each department in different formats
- Reports are often contradictory
- Data is 2-3 days delayed
- Cannot answer basic operational questions like "What is our network no-show rate this week?" without making phone calls

**Business Impact:**
- Slow decision-making at leadership level
- Inability to respond quickly to operational issues
- Misaligned priorities across departments

**Solution Area:** Unified real-time dashboards, automated reporting, consolidated data warehouse

---

### 6. **Regulatory Complexity (HIPAA + UK GDPR)**
**Problem:**
- Two distinct legal frameworks govern patient data
- Access logs and audit trails kept separately in each EHR
- Incomplete audit trails across systems
- Patient data requests require manual compilation from multiple systems

**Business Impact:**
- Risk of regulatory violations and fines
- Compliance burden on small IT team
- Slow response to regulatory requests (breach notifications, data subject requests)

**Solution Area:** Centralized compliance monitoring dashboard, consolidated audit trails, automated patient data export, compliance risk scoring

---

### 7. **Manual HR and Workforce Processes**
**Problem:**
- Clinical recruitment takes **47 days average** to close (vs. 27 days industry standard)
- Credential verification is completely manual
- Continuing Medical Education (CME) tracking done in spreadsheets
- Onboarding is unstructured
- No centralized HR portal

**Business Impact:**
- Clinician turnover risk
- Operational gaps when positions open
- Compliance risk (CME hours legally required for license maintenance)

**Solution Area:** Internal HR portal, automated credential verification, CME tracking with expiration alerts, automated onboarding checklists, HR KPI dashboards

## 📝 Regulatory Considerations

### HIPAA (USA)
- Protected Health Information (PHI) must be encrypted at rest and in transit
- Access controls and audit logging required
- Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) needed for third-party services
- Breach notification protocol mandatory

### UK GDPR (UK)
- Data Processing Addendum (DPA) required
- Right to be forgotten and data portability
- Consent tracking and management
- Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for new processing
